# Corporate Governance Resources

## Knowledge

- [India: Companies Act 2013, India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2114/5/A2013-18.pdf)
  Official statutory compilation retrieved and relevant text inspected for lesson 0001. Read sections 166 (directors' duties), 177–178 (committees), and 179–180 (board powers and limits). Section 166 expressly includes employees, shareholders, community, and environmental protection. For lesson 0002, also inspected sections 173–175 (meetings, quorum, circulation) and 179–180 (board powers, delegation, reserved decisions). The lesson uses expressly invented spending limits rather than statutory thresholds. Check amendments and applicable rules before relying on specific requirements.
- [US: Delaware General Corporation Law, sections 141–142](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc04/index.html)
  Official statute retrieved for lesson 0001. Section 141 establishes board-directed management; section 142 addresses officers. Delaware is an important US example, not a substitute for all state laws, federal securities rules, and exchange requirements.
- [UK: Financial Reporting Council, UK Corporate Governance Code](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/corporate-governance/uk-corporate-governance-code/)
  Official guidance retrieved for lesson 0001. Useful for board leadership, division of responsibilities, audit/risk/internal control, remuneration, and comply-or-explain. Code scope matters: not every UK company is covered.
- [Germany: German Corporate Governance Code, foreword](https://www.dcgk.de/en/code/current-version/preamble.html)
  Official code retrieved for lesson 0001. Explains the dual management/supervisory-board system, stakeholder interests, and employee participation. Do not generalize this structure to every German legal form.
- [Japan: JPX corporate governance reports](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/equities/listing/cg/01.html)
  Official exchange guidance retrieved for lesson 0001. Identifies alternative governance structures: kansayaku board, three committees, and supervisory committee. Use actual company reports for concrete comparisons.

# Primary Sources & Systems Architecture Resources

## Track 1: Corporate Governance Primary Sources

- **[India: Companies Act 2013, India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2114/5/A2013-18.pdf)**
  Official statutory compilation. Sections 166 (directors' statutory duties), 177–178 (Audit and NRC committees), 173–175 (board meetings & quorum), and 179–180 (board powers and limits).
- **[US: Delaware General Corporation Law, sections 141–142](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc04/index.html)**
  Official statute. Section 141 establishes board-directed management and committee delegation; section 142 addresses officers.
- **[UK: Financial Reporting Council, UK Corporate Governance Code](https://www.frc.org.uk/library/standards-codes-policy/corporate-governance/uk-corporate-governance-code/)**
  Official code. Division of responsibilities (Chair vs. CEO), internal control systems, and the "Comply or Explain" regime.
- **[Germany: German Corporate Governance Code, foreword](https://www.dcgk.de/en/code/current-version/preamble.html)**
  Official code. Explains the dual management/supervisory-board system (Vorstand vs. Aufsichtsrat) and employee co-determination (Mitbestimmung).
- **[Japan: JPX corporate governance reports](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/equities/listing/cg/01.html)**
  Official exchange guidance. Three-committee system, Kansayaku statutory auditor board, and governance reforms.

## Track 2: AI Agent Architecture & Systems Engineering References

- **[ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models (Yao et al.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)**
  Foundational architecture for interleaved reasoning traces, tool calling, and observation state updates.
- **[Temporal.io & Cadence Distributed Durable Execution](https://temporal.io)**
  Durable execution engine patterns: persistent state machines, deterministic replay, and fault-tolerant agent orchestration.
- **[OpenTelemetry Semantic Conventions for Generative AI](https://opentelemetry.io)**
  Standardized distributed tracing for multi-turn agent spans, tool latencies, and token cost telemetry.
- **[Deep Thinking: Architectural Dilemmas & Open Frontiers](reference/deep-thinking.html)**
  Analysis of network blindspots, idempotency keys, hierarchical task planning, context compaction, and multi-agent consensus trade-offs.

Charter & Curriculum Design

Mission: Corporate Governance, Then AI Agent Architecture

The Learning Rationale

As autonomous AI agents take on critical workplace responsibilities, engineering organizations require a rigorous way to reason about authority, boundaries, delegation, and retained liability. We intentionally structure this journey in two sequential phases: first master corporate governance itself, then apply those lessons directly to designing resilient AI agent architectures.

Track 1: Corporate Governance Foundations

  • 01

    Governance vs. Management & Company Separateness

    Ground company separateness, and distinguish governance from management, compliance, and operations.

  • 02

    Authority Before Votes & Decision Criteria

    Separate share ownership votes from board votes; establish delegation limits and conflict overrides.

  • 03

    Fiduciary Duties & Independent Oversight

    Distinguish Duty of Care from Duty of Loyalty; understand the role of Independent Directors on the Audit Committee.

  • 04

    Comparative Governance Models

    Compare India (promoter blockholders) against the US (dispersed ownership), the UK (comply or explain), and Germany (two-tier co-determination).

  • 05

    Retained Accountability in Delegation

    Establish the foundational legal axiom: an organization can delegate execution, but leadership retains ultimate fiduciary accountability.

Track 2: AI Agent Architecture Design

  • A1

    The Cognitive Core: Loops & State Machines

    Design deterministic finite state machines, step budgets, and loop deduplication to prevent unconstrained agent failures.

  • A2

    Tooling, Sandboxing & Protocol Design

    Enforce JSON schemas, capability-based security, isolated sandboxed runtimes, and idempotent tool execution.

  • A3

    Memory Systems & Context Retrieval

    Manage context compaction, working scratchpads, and persistent episodic/semantic vector storage.

  • A4

    Multi-Agent Topologies & Orchestration

    Implement hierarchical leader-worker, blackboard, and pipeline topologies while preventing deadlocks and sycophancy.

  • A5

    Enterprise Guardrails, HITL Gateways & Telemetry

    Build real-time circuit breakers, policy enforcement engines (Cedar/OPA), human-in-the-loop gateways, and OpenTelemetry tracing.

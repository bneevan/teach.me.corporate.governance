Primary Sources & Architectural References

1. Track 1: Corporate Governance Primary Sources

  • India: Companies Act 2013 (Official India Code) Sections 166 (directors' statutory duties), 177–178 (Audit and NRC committees), 173–175 (board meetings & quorum), and 179–180 (board powers & restrictions). ↗
  • United States: Delaware General Corporation Law Section 141 (board-directed management and committee delegation) & Section 142 (officers). ↗
  • United Kingdom: Financial Reporting Council (UK Corporate Governance Code) Division of responsibilities (Chair vs. CEO), internal control systems, and the "Comply or Explain" regime. ↗
  • Germany: German Corporate Governance Code (DCGK) Dual-board framework (Vorstand management board vs. Aufsichtsrat supervisory board) and employee co-determination (Mitbestimmung). ↗
  • Japan: JPX Corporate Governance Code & Guidelines Three-committee system, Kansayaku statutory auditor board, and ongoing governance modernization. ↗

2. Track 2: AI Agent Architecture & Systems References

  • ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models (Yao et al.) The foundational paper establishing interleaved thought-action-observation traces in LLMs. ↗
  • Temporal.io & Cadence Architecture Durable execution engines providing deterministic event logging and fault-tolerant agent state machine orchestration. ↗
  • OpenTelemetry Distributed Tracing Standard Standardized telemetry format for recording multi-turn agent spans, tool latency, and prompt injection detection. ↗
  • Architectural Dilemmas & Deep Scenarios Explores idempotency keys, hierarchical task planning, context compaction, and multi-agent consensus trade-offs. ↗

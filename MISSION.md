# Mission: Corporate Governance, Then AI Agent Architecture Design

## The Flow
1. **Track 1: Corporate Governance Foundations** (Completed)
   - Understand company separateness, authority allocation, and limits on executive power.
   - Master the difference between shareholding votes and board seat votes.
   - Distinguish the Duty of Care from the Duty of Loyalty, and evaluate Independent Director oversight on the Audit Committee.
   - Compare India (promoter blockholders) against the US (dispersed owners), the UK (comply or explain), and Germany (two-tier co-determination).
   - Establish the legal reality of retained fiduciary accountability in delegation.

2. **Track 2: AI Agent Architecture Design** (In progress: 1/5 completed · Lesson A02 up next)
   - Cognitive Core: Finite state machines, ReAct loops, step budgets, and preventing infinite token burn.
   - Tooling & Sandboxing: Capability-based access control, isolated execution environments, and side-effect idempotency.
   - Memory Architecture: Context compaction, structured scratchpads, and persistent episodic/semantic vector retrieval.
   - Multi-Agent Orchestration: Hierarchical leader-worker, blackboard, and pipeline topologies without deadlocks or sycophancy.
   - Enterprise Guardrails & Observability: Policy engines (Cedar/OPA), circuit breakers, HITL gateways, and OpenTelemetry.

## Constraints & Principles
- First establish corporate governance fundamentals, then apply that foundation to architecting autonomous agent systems.
- Use India as the comparative reference point for legal governance.
- Focus on practical systems architecture, state machines, and real-world failure modes.
- Educational overview and systems engineering reference, not legal advice.

# Mission: Corporate Governance, Then AI-Agent Governance

## Why
As AI agents take on more workplace activity, develop a grounded way to reason about the authority, boundaries, and accountability of companies. First learn corporate governance itself, then apply that foundation to delegated AI-agent work.

## Success looks like
- Explain governance versus management, compliance, and operations using a concrete company scenario.
- Map shareholders, directors, committees, and executives to their powers and responsibilities.
- Compare India with the US, UK, Germany, Japan, and China without treating East and West as uniform models.
- Distinguish legal requirements, governance codes, ownership patterns, and actual practice.
- Evaluate an AI-agent delegation scenario for authorization, oversight, escalation, and retained accountability.

## Constraints
- Start with a beginner primer and diagnostic quiz; prior knowledge is not yet assessed.
- Use India as the comparative reference point.
- Use short, source-grounded lessons and interactive feedback.

## Out of scope
- Engineering issue-tracker and triage setup.
- Treating this educational material as jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
- Designing AI-agent governance before establishing corporate-governance fundamentals.

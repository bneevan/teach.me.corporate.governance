The Authority Check: From Boardrooms to AI Agents

First identify who may decide. Then determine how that person or body must decide. Check substantive lawfulness as well.

Part 1: The 5-Step Corporate Authority Check

  1. Classify the action: What is being authorized: a routine purchase, debt borrowing, a major acquisition, or a related-party deal?
  2. Check sources and limits of authority: Applicable law (Companies Act), articles of association, reserved matters, board resolutions, and valid delegations. An internal company policy cannot legalize an act prohibited by law.
  3. Check special triggers: Conflicts of interest, related parties, monetary thresholds, budget limits, and mandatory committee or shareholder approvals. More than one approval may be required.
  4. Follow the correct process: Notice, information pack, quorum, eligible participation, voting thresholds, and formal minutes. Quorum and voting threshold are distinct legal concepts.
  5. Do not confuse the hats: Ownership rights, a director's board vote, and executive authority are distinct. A 60% shareholding does not turn 1 board vote into 60% of board votes.

Part 2: Direct Translation to AI Agent Systems Architecture

When building enterprise agent workflows, the corporate authority check translates directly into systems engineering guardrails:

Corporate Step Corporate Legal Rule Agent Systems Architecture Implementation
1. Classify Action Distinguish routine operational expense from strategic corporate commitments. Tool Capability Classification: Strictly isolate Read-Only tools (search, DB queries) from Destructive/Mutating tools (payment APIs, database writes, git pushes).
2. Check Limits Management acts within formal financial delegations (e.g., CEO limit ₹5 lakh). Programmatic Boundary Controls: Hard caps on token budgets, API request rate limiters, spending quotas, and whitelisted counterparty domains.
3. Special Triggers A related-party deal suspends routine delegation and escalates to Audit Committee. Automated Policy Interceptors: If an agent detects a conflict, low confidence score, or high dollar amount, execution is halted and routed to a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) gateway.
4. Correct Process A board vote is void without notice, quorum, and statutory minutes. Pre-Execution Invariants: The agent cannot call tools unless input parameters pass strict Pydantic/JSON Schema validation and idempotency key verification.
5. Hat Separation Promoter ownership does not override fiduciary board duties. Retained Accountability: The agent executes tasks; human directors and executive sponsors retain non-delegable legal liability for outcomes.

India Statutory Reference

Companies Act 2013: sections 166 (duties), 173–175 (meetings, quorum, circulation), 177 (Audit Committee), 179 (board powers), and 180 (restrictions on board powers).

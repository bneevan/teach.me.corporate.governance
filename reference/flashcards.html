CG-001 · Conflicts: managed, not erased

A director's relative offers the best price and quality. Does that remove the conflict? Must the company reject the transaction?

Reveal answer

No to both. The relationship—and therefore the conflict—remains. The company may potentially proceed if the transaction is lawful and the conflict is properly disclosed and managed, with appropriate review, participation restrictions, required approvals, and documentation under applicable rules.

A company may accept a transaction involving a conflict without accepting conflicted decision-making.

Remember: commercial merits and decision-process integrity are separate questions. A good deal does not excuse bypassing required safeguards; a proper process cannot legalize a prohibited transaction.

India source for later detail: Companies Act 2013, particularly sections 177, 184, and 188. Applicable rules, exceptions, company type, and listing requirements must also be checked.
